About Guardian Angel

A family-owned and operated company serving Columbus with care and compassion.

Guardian Angel Home Health & Medical Transport was founded to serve older adults and families across Columbus, Ohio with respectful in-home support, dependable transportation, and the kind of personal attention that only a family-owned business can provide.

Our story

Care that feels personal because the business is personal.

As a family-owned and operated business, Guardian Angel believes care should never feel distant or transactional. Families deserve clear communication, dependable service, and support that honors the dignity of the person receiving care.

We are proud to be based in Columbus and to serve local households with private pay home care, companionship, help with everyday routines, and medical transportation that keeps appointments and recovery plans on track.

Care philosophy

The values that guide Guardian Angel every day.

Dignity first

Every client deserves to feel respected, heard, and cared for with patience and kindness.

Dependable communication

Local commitment

Being based in Columbus means staying close to the community and serving local households with pride.
