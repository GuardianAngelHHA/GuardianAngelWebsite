VA Benefits

Are you eligible to receive up to $2,500 per month through a VA benefit to help offset the cost of care services?

Fill out the questionnaire below and one of our representatives will contact you to discuss the VA benefit and your eligibility. This screening is designed for families who want to explore whether the Aid and Attendance benefit may be a fit.

VA Aid and Attendance

Start a quick eligibility questionnaire.

If you are a Veteran, a living spouse, or a surviving spouse, this benefit may help offset the cost of care services at home. Start the questionnaire below to answer one question at a time.

Question 1 of 10 Veteran or spouse status
1. Are you a Veteran, living spouse, or surviving spouse of a Veteran over age 65?
2. Do you need some assistance at home with activities of daily living?
3. Do you have monthly household combined income from all sources below these amounts?

Couples: $3,000. Single Veteran: $2,600. Surviving Spouse: $2,000. If you are spending money on care services right now, your income could be higher and you may still qualify. If you are living in assisted or independent living, or paying for care now, you may be eligible even with income above these amounts.

4. Do you have less than $129,600 in retirement accounts, annuities, stocks, bonds, and cash in the bank?

Your home value and personal property are exempt.

5. Was the surviving spouse married to the Veteran at the time of the Veteran's death?
6. Did the Veteran serve at least 1 day of active duty during a wartime period?

National Guard or Reserves do not count. Qualifying periods include: World War II, December 1941 to December 1946; Korean War, June 1950 to January 1955; Vietnam War, February 1961 to August 1964 if served in Vietnam, or September 1964 to May 1975 in or outside of Vietnam; Gulf War, 1990 to present.

7. Is the benefit applicant receiving monthly money from the VA, or have they applied for money from the VA, for wounds suffered during military service or for a pension benefit?
8. Is the applicant currently living at home and planning to stay there for at least a year, or do they have a discharge date to return home from their current facility?
9. Best time to call

Check any that work best for you.
