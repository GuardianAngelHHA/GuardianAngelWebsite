Employee Onboarding

Complete your employee forms.

Fill out the forms here and download completed copies of the official PDFs. Complete them after accepting a job offer, then sign and submit them through the secure onboarding form below.

Submit onboarding forms only after accepting a job offer. Your information is used in this browser to prepare the PDFs. It is sent to Guardian Angel only when you choose to submit the forms. After accepting an offer

Submit your onboarding forms

Download the completed PDFs below, sign them using Guardian Angel’s approved process, then attach all three here. Form I-9 Section 1 must be completed after you accept an offer and no later than your first day of work. Bring your chosen acceptable documents to Guardian Angel so the employer can complete its review and Section 2.

01

Federal tax withholding

Form W-4

Enter your personal information and withholding choices. The fields below prepare the official 2026 W-4 PDF for download.

Step 1: Personal information
Filing status (choose one)
Steps 2–4: Complete only if they apply

If you claim exemption, do not complete Steps 2–4. Your signature and date are left blank for signing through Guardian Angel’s approved process.

Use the IRS W-4 instructions for withholding choices. Do not enter information you are unsure about.

02

Employment eligibility

Form I-9

Enter your information for Section 1 after accepting the job offer and no later than your first day of work. Your employer completes Section 2 after reviewing acceptable documents.

Section 1: Employee information
Citizenship or immigration status (choose one)

The employee signature and date are left blank. This download page does not verify identity for an electronic I-9 signature. Sign Section 1 in person or through Guardian Angel’s approved I-9 process. If a preparer or translator helped, have them complete Supplement A on page 3. You choose which acceptable documents to present; do not email document images.

Read USCIS I-9 guidance.

03

Ohio tax withholding

Ohio IT 4

Complete the fields below to prepare the official Ohio IT 4 PDF. The signature line is left blank for signing through Guardian Angel’s approved process.

Section I: Personal information
Section II: Claiming withholding exemptions
Section III: Withholding waiver, if applicable

Check a box only if it applies to you. Review the official instructions before claiming a waiver.

If a waiver applies, Ohio’s instructions say to complete Sections I, III, and IV only; Section II will be left blank in your PDF.

Section IV: Signature

By signing, I declare under penalties of perjury that this certificate is true, correct, and complete.

The signature and date lines are left blank. Sign using Guardian Angel’s approved process.

The official Ohio IT 4 includes instructions and other details. Review Ohio employer withholding information.
