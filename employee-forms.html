Employee Onboarding

Complete your employee forms.

Fill out the forms below on your device, save a copy for your records, and contact Guardian Angel for the approved secure way to return them.

Your information stays on this device while you fill out this page. This page does not upload or email your information. Do not send completed forms or identity documents by regular email.
01

Federal tax withholding

Form W-4

Complete the current 2026 Employee’s Withholding Certificate. The PDF can be filled in a compatible browser or PDF reader.

Open fillable W-4

After completing it, use your PDF reader’s Save or Download option. You can also view the latest W-4 at IRS.gov.

02

Employment eligibility

Form I-9

Complete Section 1 after accepting the job offer and no later than your first day of work. Your employer completes Section 2 after reviewing acceptable documents.

Open fillable I-9

You choose which acceptable documents to present. Do not email document images. Read USCIS I-9 guidance.

03

Ohio tax withholding

Ohio IT 4

Complete the fields below. When you select Print / Save as PDF, choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog to keep a copy. You may also print and sign the form.

Section I: Personal information
Section II: Claiming withholding exemptions
Section III: Withholding waiver, if applicable

Check a box only if it applies to you. Review the official instructions before claiming a waiver.

Section IV: Signature

By signing, I declare under penalties of perjury that this certificate is true, correct, and complete.

The official Ohio IT 4 includes instructions and other details. Open the official Ohio IT 4 PDF or review Ohio employer withholding information.

Returning your forms

Need a secure way to submit them?

Contact Guardian Angel to arrange an approved secure return method or an in-person handoff. For Form I-9, bring your chosen acceptable documents so the employer can complete its review and Section 2.
